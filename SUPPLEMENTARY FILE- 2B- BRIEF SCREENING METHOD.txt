SCREENING METHOD
Project: Fn/Pg and oral squamous cell carcinoma literature screening
Input workbook: OSCC_FnPg_Final_Deduplicated_Records_CLEAN(1).xlsx
Input worksheet: Final_Deduplicated
Input records: 2,583
Unique records retained by this screening rule: 991
Records not retained by this rule: 1,592

1. SCREENING RULE
Screen records for the occurrence of at least one of these five phrases in the available searchable metadata:
1) Fusobacterium nucleatum
2) Porphyromonas gingivalis
3) oral squamous cell carcinoma
4) oral carcinogenesis
5) oral microbiome dysbiosis

A record is retained if ANY ONE OR MORE of the five phrases is found (Boolean OR rule).
The user's spelling "Oral Squamous Cell Carcinome" was standardized to "oral squamous cell carcinoma".

2. FIELDS SEARCHED
Title; Abstract; Author_Keywords; Index_Keywords; MeSH_Terms.
The supplied workbook has no full-text field or full-text documents. Therefore, full texts were NOT searched in this screening pass.
The keyword and MeSH columns are present but entirely empty in this input workbook, so actual matches came from available Title and/or Abstract text.
Records with missing abstracts were still assessed using the other available fields.

3. MATCHING LOGIC
- Case-insensitive phrase matching.
- One or more whitespace characters are accepted between words in each phrase.
- Word boundaries are used to avoid matching the phrase as part of a longer word.
- No synonyms, abbreviations (e.g., OSCC), stemming, inferred relevance, or additional organism names were added.
- A record is counted once in the eligible output even if it matches multiple terms.
- The per-term counts below overlap and must not be added together to obtain the unique eligible count.

4. REPRODUCIBLE REGULAR EXPRESSIONS
Fusobacterium nucleatum:       r'\bfusobacterium\s+nucleatum\b'       (case-insensitive)
Porphyromonas gingivalis:      r'\bporphyromonas\s+gingivalis\b'      (case-insensitive)
oral squamous cell carcinoma:  r'\boral\s+squamous\s+cell\s+carcinoma\b' (case-insensitive)
oral carcinogenesis:           r'\boral\s+carcinogenesis\b'           (case-insensitive)
oral microbiome dysbiosis:     r'\boral\s+microbiome\s+dysbiosis\b'  (case-insensitive)

Equivalent Boolean expression:
("Fusobacterium nucleatum" OR "Porphyromonas gingivalis" OR
 "oral squamous cell carcinoma" OR "oral carcinogenesis" OR
 "oral microbiome dysbiosis")

6. IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
This is a literal phrase-based screening pass according to the specified five-term rule. It is not a full-text eligibility assessment and does not establish that each retained record is scientifically relevant to Fn/Pg mechanisms or oral carcinogenesis. In particular, the broad OR rule retains a record that mentions "oral squamous cell carcinoma" even if neither target organism is mentioned. Full-text screening and substantive eligibility review should be conducted separately if required by the review protocol.
